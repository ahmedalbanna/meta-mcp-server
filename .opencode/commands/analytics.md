Analyze performance metrics for Instagram and ad campaigns.

Without arguments: show Instagram account overview (followers, reach, engagement).

With arguments:
- `/analytics instagram` — Instagram account insights
- `/analytics post [ID]` — specific post insights
- `/analytics campaign [ID]` — campaign performance
- `/analytics report` — generate full CSV report
- `/analytics top` — show top 5 performing posts
- `/analytics competitors` — run competitor analysis

Use these tools:
- `get_instagram_account_insights` — account-level metrics
- `get_instagram_media_insights` — post-level metrics
- `get_campaign_insights` — campaign metrics
- `generate_campaign_report` — export to CSV
- `generate_instagram_report` — Instagram export

Always compare to previous period and provide insights.
